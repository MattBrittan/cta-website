Our place

High-country habitat worth fighting for.

Formed in 2019, the Craigieburn Trapping Alliance brings people together to reduce the pressure of introduced predators on this remarkable landscape.

Our area stretches from the Cass River to Lake Lyndon, taking in beech forest, tussocklands, braided rivers, lakes and alpine environments of deep cultural importance to Ngāi Tahu.

Get involved

Lend a hand in the high country.

Monthly volunteer days run on weekends during the trapping season, from September to May. New volunteers can learn alongside experienced trappers before taking on a line of their own.
